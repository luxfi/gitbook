# Real world assets (RWAs)

LUX continues to make investments in emerging market projects leveraging in-house expertise and global networks. For example, LUX can facilitate gold transactions between nations, and is financially engineered to provide leverage to further augment resource extraction. There are countless sovereign nations with proven natural resources that are unobtainable due to financial constraints, a problem that can be remedied by tokenizing assets to fund the development of resource extraction infrastructure through LUX. The use cases of blockchain in this field are ubiquitous and untapped. LUX is the first major entrant and expects to be the market leader in application of blockchain technology to natural resource and commodity trade in emerging markets, advantaged by its MTL.
