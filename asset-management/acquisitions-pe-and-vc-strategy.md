# Acquisitions, PE & VC Strategy

Lux is exploring synergistic opportunities for partnerships, mergers, and acquisitions, specifically in FinTech and decentralized banking sectors. The purpose of this is to go to market with Treasury as a Service (TaaS), Clearing, and Digital Banking platforms in 2023 to bolster financial technology offerings and optimize Lux’s operational scalability. Targets include NeoBanks, DeFi lending platforms, Exchanges, and other financial service companies that offer crypto and fiat integrated services with licenses and an emphasis on ownership of intellectual property and consumer data.&#x20;

Lux will employ a multitude of additional strategies including investments in equities, fixed income, commercial real estate, commodities, options, futures, algorithmic trading, and  many others to properly scale and diversify the Asset Management vertical synergistically with other lines of business.
