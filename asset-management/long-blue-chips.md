# Long "Blue Chips"

Lux will allocate a portion of funds to a diversified portfolio of the top performing and largest market cap cryptocurrencies. This includes investments in Bitcoin, Ethereum, Avalanche, Solana, Harmony, Terra, Ripple, Cardano, BNB, and many others. This strategy can be leveraged, which Lux will employ in certain environments, while in others, hedged against volatility and downside risk. The thesis for this strategy is as follows: exponential increases in network adoption, in conjunction with an influx of institutional investment, will drive up valuations in the short and medium-term, despite looming regulation, which will have a positive long-term impact.
