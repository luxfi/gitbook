# Staking

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-08-14 at 9.24.19 PM.png>)

**Staking** provides an additional means by which investors can make passive income. Staking refers to the holding of cryptocurrency funds in a designated wallet, thus supporting the functionality of a blockchain system. On the Lux Blockchain, two tokens can be staked, LUX and GLUX, which earn compound interest as a reward from the network. One benefit for validator owners is that revenue earned in LUX earns an APY of 111.11% for being staked. That reward is given in GLUX, which rebases at an APY of 111,111%. This return is due to the nature of compound interest, which is calculated every 30 minutes, replicating the OHM financial model. Validator NFTs include a 10M LUX token bond that further bolsters ROI.
