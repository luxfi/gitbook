# Lux Wallet

![The Lux Wallet technology suite is a state of the art payment management system that was created to significantly reduce the cost of moving money to family and friends. Lux Wallet has created a PayPal type mobile wallet account system that is equivalent to a virtual savings account. It has also integrated credit and debit cards that are equivalent to a virtual checking account. By combining and integrating these two products, Lux Wallet has built a very powerful mobile commerce product suite that has led to an extremely cost efficient money movement strategy within the U.S. as well as internationally.](../.gitbook/assets/14.png)
