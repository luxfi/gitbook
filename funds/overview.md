# Lux Fund of Funds

Lux Fund is a Fund of Funds multi-strategy asset management fund domiciled in Luxembourg. Through a protected cell structure with scalable and holistic back-office functionality, Lux Fund enables investment management and capital allocation decisions to be automated and scale to support unlimited assets. This fund will service a multitude of investor profiles with differing financial goals and risk appetites through segregated allocation of assets into a diversified portfolio projecting attractive returns while protecting principle against notable risks. The Asset Management vertical integrates synergistically with Lux’s transactional capabilities and in alignment with its blockchain expertise.&#x20;

Lux is utilizing a strategic organizational structure consisting of a Lux Partners Ltd., the parent company domiciled in the Isle of Man, as well as entities throughout the Caymans, Luxembourg, The U.S., Dubai, and Mauritius. This provides access to desirable markets and financial instruments in meticulous consideration of regulatory and tax implications. The fund, utilizing a protected cell company within Lux Fund’s established fund of funds infrastructure, will scale multiple strategies consisting of both decentralized and traditional finance instruments.
